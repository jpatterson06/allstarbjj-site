## A Guy Walked In Last Week With a Contract He Didn't Understand

He lives in Kenilworth, signed up at a strip-mall "kickboxing" place over the summer, and got locked into a 24-month agreement with an early termination fee higher than what he'd pay out the contract. He came to us because his shins hurt from hitting a heavy bag wrong for four months and nobody ever corrected him. That's not an isolated story. It's the norm for about 80% of the "Muay Thai" gyms within fifteen minutes of here.

I'm not writing this to trash every gym in Union County. I'm writing it because October is when people get serious again — summer's over, the kids are back in school, and adults start looking for a real program instead of whatever got them through August. If you're about to hand someone your credit card, you should know what you're actually paying for.

## Lineage Isn't a Marketing Word, It's a Paper Trail

I'm a Renzo Gracie black belt. That means something specific: there's a lineage from Carlos Gracie down through Renzo, and I can tell you who promoted me and when. Ask any instructor the same question. "Who's your black belt under, and can I look them up?" A real instructor answers in one sentence. A McDojo owner gets vague, talks about "my own system," or shows you a certificate from a weekend seminar.

Same goes for Muay Thai specifically. Ask if the striking coach has actually competed, not just trained. I went 6-3 as a pro — IFL, Bellator, UWC light heavyweight title — and every technique I teach got tested against someone trying to knock my head off, not just drilled in the mirror. That's the difference between a coach who's read about getting hit and one who's been hit.

## Watch What Happens in Week Three, Not Week One

Any gym can fake a good first class. Energy's high, the instructor's paying attention, everybody claps. The real test is what the program looks like once you're not a new sign-up anymore.

At our Union location, adult Muay Thai runs structured rounds — pad work, clinch work, controlled sparring once you've got the basics down. You will get corrected on your hands when they drop. You will get told your hips aren't turning over on your kicks. If a gym just lets you flail on a heavy bag for 45 minutes set to music and calls that a class, you're paying for a workout, not an education. Nothing wrong with a workout. Just don't pay striking-program prices for it.

## The Contract Is the Biggest Tell

Here's something nobody tells you walking in: month-to-month means the gym has to earn your business every single month. A 2-year lock-in means they don't. If a place is pushing you hard to sign long-term before you've even taken a class, ask yourself why they need that kind of insurance on a product they claim is this good.

We run trial classes with zero pressure specifically because I don't want anyone in a Kenilworth or Springfield zip code signing up out of social pressure in a sales office. You should decide after you've thrown pads for forty minutes, not after someone with a quota walks you through a whiteboard.

## What a First Real Class Actually Looks Like

You show up, we get you wraps and gloves if you don't have your own. Warm-up is short, maybe eight minutes, nothing that leaves you throwing up before you've learned a single strike. Then we break down two or three techniques — a jab-cross combo, a teep, maybe a basic low kick depending on the group. You hit pads with a partner, I'm walking around correcting grips and footwork. Last fifteen minutes is controlled drilling, no one's getting kneed in the face in their first week.

That's it. No forms to memorize, no belt ceremony, no pitch at the end. If you're a 35-year-old who hasn't done any kind of training since high school sports, this is built for you. Nobody's expecting you to look like a fighter in week one.

Kenilworth's a small town. People talk. If a gym's cutting corners, word gets around fast, and it should. The flip side: if you're vetting a gym and the only reviews you find are five-star with no specifics, or the gym won't tell you their instructor's lineage, that's data. Weight it accordingly.
